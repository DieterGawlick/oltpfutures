We have 
	Data that represents our current state: our resources
	Data that needs to be acted on: Messages

Messaging is the essential component
	All messages are immutable from the moment they are sent
		Immutable means "tamper resistant" (not "tamper proof") 
			Any change is flagged and obvious (not tamper resistant)
	The message is saved as the next path of an immutable time series
		Blockchains provide a mechanism for storing the time series
	Only then is the message acted on 
	A message is a database entry that needs attention -- that needs to be processed
		An unprocessed message has the attention index
	In a B2B transaction what the partners discuss are the messages
		And, of course, the impact of the messages on the partners resources

The protocol has to establish what the partners agree on -- what data means the same to both parties
	Both partners have a shared understanding on what the data structures in the message mean	
		Internally, each partner has its own understanding of what the data means	
	Data structures in business are hierarchical
		Our focus is on the top level
		Moves down through the detail

From the initial request from one partner to the money dispensed by the other partner
	If everything is recoverable than everything is auditable
	The application not only continues running but can report on its failure

Messages are pieces that direct each partner's workflow
	a set of action steps
	each step has to be recoverable (which is our definition of transaction)
	each message for each step must be delivered exactly one

Workflows happen over time
	A transaction is a moment in time
		The resources at the current moment
		The resources of an expected future (escrow)
	Escrow in transactions
		Escrow gives all the partners the opportunity to make sure the transaction is valid
		Determine the state of the current resources
	That future may be disrupted by a new message
		Escrow is discarded and partners return to their starting position

Transaction at the core
	Messaging over top to connect the players

The bank has custody/ownership of your money -- the resource being managed by the partners
	Once the bank gives you give the cash, the bank transfers custody/ownership from the bank to you


What did we learn about messages?
	Are visible to both partners
	Are critical 
	Must be retained
	Must be visible

What did we learn about workflows?
	Are internal to the partner
		Invisible and immaterial to the other partner
	Are tied together through immutable messages

In today's terminology:
	A process can be represented as traversing a graph
		IMS provides the foundation for a recoverable process
		Transaction are nodes
		Messages are the edges that join the nodes
	Since the messages are immutable, we can "re-transverse" through the graph at any time

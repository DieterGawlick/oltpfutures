Log Write Ahead Dataset
	The road to LSM

Write out the journal as fast as possible
	Get to "recoverability" as fast as possible
	Once the transaction is recoverable, the world outside the transaction can be notified

In order to use the journal, we need long blocks
	the journal has two characteristics
		When you write it
		Where you read it
	The journal is there to ensure recoverability 
	“recoverability” means “written to the journal”

Writing a long block (16K) takes awhile - you have to wait a long time
	Plus seek time
	Further slowed down waiting for the sector to come around
		Perhaps moving to the right cylinder
	First step: Dedicate a disk to the journal
	Seek time (on average): half a rotation
	Altogether 30 millisecond

Solution
	Reduce search, seek, and write
	Just take the next block -- seek time is now just half a block

Don't write out the whole journal
	Write out notes
	Write out a short block (2K) and just leave the rest blank
	Total time now down to 2 milliseconds

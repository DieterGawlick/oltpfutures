The saga model
	A set of related transactions
	Semantics
		"What do you have"/"Find availability"
		"Hold things"/"Reserve"
		"Commit"

Saga of planning (assembling the resources for) a trip
	Find a hotel
	Put it on hold
		Infrastructure: put a subscription on the resource to be notified
	Find a flight
	Put it on hold
		Infrastructure: put a subscription on the resource to be notified
	Find a different hotel
	Put it on hold
		Infrastructure: put a subscription on the resource to be notified
	Find a different flight
	Put it on hold
		Infrastructure: put a subscription on the resource to be notified
	Assemble a saga of a flight and hotel
		Only use resources that are still available
	Execute the saga (all taken care of by the infrastructure)
		Commit the selected hotel/flight

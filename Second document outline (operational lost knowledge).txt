: in under 1,000 words

Rule:
	The application, can't share anything to the outside world (e.g. the client) until it is recoverable
		You don't send a message to another participant until the data is recoverable

Sagas
	Planning phase -> secure specific resources required for this specific activity
		What is available for me activity
		Put some resources on hold (not transaction because holds go on for too long)
		Eventually, convert some "on hold" resources to future reservations
		Release the unreserved "on hold" resources

	Execution phase
		Consuming the reserved resources (and making them 'consumed resources')
		Responding to changes in reserved resources: Situational analysis
			 Re-planning all or part of the saga	
				Putting future resources on hold
				Reserving some "on hold" resources
				Release previously reserved resources
				Release the unreserved "on hold" resources

	The reserved resources are a "forward chain" and must be mutable
		The consumed resources are an immutable "backward chain"

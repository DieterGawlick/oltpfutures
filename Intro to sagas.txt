Saga
	The goal is to provide an infrastructure for performing series of activities over time
		Make it transparent to the developer

	Initially, a saga was one transaction after another
		If you run into a problem, you do a compensation
		But, over time, compensation is sometimes impossible 
			if I go on a trip, complete the first leg and can't do the second leg, I can't undo the first leg

	Sagas evolved by recognizing temporality 
		A process occurring over time
			Not one atomic action or series of atomic actions
			But a series of actions that depend on each other
			Distributed in time: you did something, you did something later
		Still message based
		Transactions vs. saga: 
			Transactions have no time
				No future or past: done in one action
				You can do something before or after but not "in between"
			Sagas have temporality
				A future: What we're planning to do in the future
				A past: What we've done so far in the process
				Where we are in the process

	Essential where the items in the resource aren't commutative
		Given a goal, plan the steps in the saga
			Time-based
			A schedule
		Reserve resource for the required period in the schedule
			But the plan and the requirement for the resource may change
			Depends on the availability of the resource at the time
			Depends on the need for the resource when the time comes
		To reserve means
			Send a message to Find and hold
			Response: 
				I can hold it for x period of time
				Get a subscription for updates to changes in the resource
			Repeat for other resources required
			If all desired reservations for the necessary resources can be made: 
				Commit the desired reservation (payment)
				A contract: 
					One party commits to delivering the resource at the scheduled time
					Renews or creates the subscription to deliver updates

		New compensation mechanism: a "clean up"
			To release resources on hold during planning
			To release resources committed during execution
			To flag when resources can't be released
